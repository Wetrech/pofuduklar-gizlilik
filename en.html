Pofuduklar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026
Publisher: Wetrech Studio (Furkan Buğday). Contact: habbe.uygulama@outlook.com

1. In short

The game does not ask for an account and does not collect your name, e-mail or device ID. When the internet is available it connects to our server for only three things: online matches and trophies in Dünya (section 3a), checking that a Diamond purchase is genuine (section 3), and anonymous usage counters that you can turn off (section 3b). Ads are shown only at the end of a match, and only if you ask for one. Adventure and Home work without the internet.

2. Information stored on your device

  • The game stores some information only on your own device: game progress, plush collection, settings (sound, language) and an age group.
  • A match left unfinished is stored on the device so that it can be resumed where it stopped, and is deleted when the match ends.
  • The age group comes from the birth year chosen at first launch or from Google Play's age signal (section 4). The birth year itself is not stored. The age group is used only to choose age-appropriate settings.
  • We cannot see this information. If Android backup is on, Android may back it up to your own Google account (section 3).

3. Ads, purchases, backup, accounts

  • In-app purchases: Diamond packs are bought through Google Play. Your payment details stay with Google; we never see them. When a purchase is complete, the game sends the purchase token issued by Google Play and the product name to our server. The server asks Google Play whether the purchase is genuine and, if it is, tells Google Play it has been delivered; only then are the Diamonds added. So that the same purchase is never counted twice, an irreversible hash of the token, the product name and the date are kept for one year. The order number, your name, e-mail, Google account, IP address and device details are not stored. For players under 13, or when no age has been given, a short check that an adult solves is shown before a purchase.
  • Checking that the game is official: When you enter Dünya and when a purchase is checked, the game asks Google Play for an integrity token (Google Play Integrity). The token tells us whether the game is unmodified, whether it was obtained from Google Play and whether the device passes a basic security check. Our server has Google verify the token, uses the result only for that request and does not store it. Google processes some information about the device under its own terms when it creates the token.
  • Rewarded ads: An ad is shown only at the end of a match, and only if you tap "Watch an ad", through Google AdMob; nothing is loaded before you tap, and there are no ads in Home matches or anywhere else in the game. Child-directed settings are on for every user: no personalised or remarketing ads, no advertising ID is sent (the app does not even have the advertising ID permission), no third-party ad measurement, and ad content is limited to the most general rating (G). When an ad is requested, Google processes, according to its own documentation: the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate the general location), interactions with the ad (views, taps, video views), diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the ads software, and device identifiers such as the app set ID. This data is encrypted in transit. How Google processes it: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Backup: If Android backup is on, Android may back up your game data (progress, collection, Diamond balance, cosmetics, settings) to your own Google account, and it comes back on a new phone or after a reinstall. We cannot access that backup; you can turn it off in Android settings.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Online play is only in Dünya and needs no account (section 3a).

3a. Dünya (online play)

  • The game connects to the internet only when you enter Dünya. Adventure and Home keep working offline.
  • For an online match we send only: a random game ID created at install (not your name, e-mail or phone number), the plush you picked, the four toys in your toy bag and a nickname chosen from a fixed list, your shot commands during the match and preset emotes.
  • So that a match can carry on where it left off if the connection drops, the match state is written to the server's temporary storage every turn. This record contains the nickname you chose from the fixed list, your plush and the toys in your bag, and which toys and maps the room has (those its creator has unlocked in the game); it contains no name, game ID, IP address or device information. When the match ends, all of its records are deleted. The server may run in Cloudflare's data centres in Europe; the match record is processed there for the duration of the match and is not stored permanently.
  • Trophies and the Dünya record: So that you can win trophies in Dünya and compare trophies with the players you play with, our server keeps a small Dünya record linked to an irreversible digest of your random game ID; the ID itself is not stored. It contains: your trophy count, a hidden skill rating worked out from your results against your opponents so that matches stay fair, your match count, your trophy level and the Trophy Path rewards you have collected, the nickname you chose from the fixed list, the date of your last match, digests of the game IDs of the players you played with in the last 30 days, and a few small numbers for the match-balance rules (how many matches in a row you have lost; on which day you last played the same players and how many matches you played together that day). Your name, e-mail, device details and IP address are not in the record. The friends table shows only the players you played with in the last 30 days, their nicknames and trophies; there are no profiles, messages or friend requests. The record is not used for advertising or marketing, not sold and not shared with anyone; it is processed only on Cloudflare's infrastructure on our behalf. If you do not play Dünya for 12 months, the record is deleted automatically. You can delete it at any time in Settings → "Delete my Dünya record" (behind a check that an adult solves for players under 13 or without a given age). The game ID stays in the game data on your device; if an Android backup is restored, your trophies come back too.
  • The server runs on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Your IP address is processed to make the connection; we do not log it.
  • To stop too many wrong room-code attempts, rooms being opened too often and counter packages being sent too often, an irreversible digest of the connection address is kept in memory for at most 15 minutes and is never written anywhere.
  • There is no chat and no free text. Players can only send preset emotes (such as a wave or a clap).
  • For players under 13, or when no age has been given, Dünya first asks for a short check that an adult solves. This check also tells the adult that a Dünya record is kept for trophies.

3b. Anonymous usage counters

To improve the game, we learn from totals which levels feel hard, which toys get used and how smoothly the game runs on phones. The game turns what happens during the day into numbers on the device (for example "Adventure 1-3 won: 2") and, when the internet is available, sends them to our server at most once a day. The package contains only these numbers, the game version, the language, the day, a rough range of how many days the game has been installed and an age group (under 13, 13 and over, not given). It contains no game ID, device ID, name, location or IP address. The server adds the package to the totals of all players as soon as it arrives and does not keep the package itself; what a single player did cannot be worked out from these totals. Totals are kept for up to two years. You can turn this off with the "Anonymous counters" switch in Settings; while it is off, nothing is sent.

4. Third parties

  • The game has no third-party analytics or crash reporting service; anonymous counters are on our own server (section 3b).
  • Rewarded ads use Google AdMob and purchases use Google Play Billing (section 3), both under Google's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Play Developer API and Google Play Integrity are used to verify purchases and to check that the game is official (section 3).
  • The Dünya server, purchase records and counter totals are processed on Cloudflare's infrastructure on our behalf; server logging is turned off.
  • At first launch the game asks the Google Play app on the device for an age range and parental approval status only (Google Play Age Signals). Google Play may ask you before sharing it. The answer is turned into an age group on the device; the game does not send it anywhere.
  • The store you downloaded the app from (Google Play) may process some download and device-stability information under its own terms and privacy policy. This processing is not under our control.

5. Children

  • Pofuduklar is suitable for players of all ages, including children.
  • Adventure and Home process no personal data. In Dünya, the IP address is processed only to make the connection, for the duration of the match. The random game ID, the nickname and match results are kept in the Dünya record for trophies and the friends table (section 3a); no profile is built for advertising or marketing. Before entering Dünya, players under 13 or without a given age get a short check that an adult solves, and that screen explains the Dünya record; if Google Play reports that a parent declined, Dünya stays closed.
  • Purchase verification and anonymous counters contain no information that identifies a person and are not used for profiling or advertising.
  • Ads are shown to everyone with child-directed settings, never personalised, and only when asked for (section 3). Before a real-money purchase, players under 13 or without a given age get a check that an adult solves.
  • If a future feature requires processing data, we will update this policy first and obtain parental consent where required.

6. Deletion

  • To delete all game data, uninstall the app or clear the app's data in your phone settings.
  • In-game Settings → "Reset progress" does the same.
  • The Dünya record is on our server, not on your device, so uninstalling the app does not delete it. You can delete it in Settings → "Delete my Dünya record", or write to us with the game code shown in Settings. It is deleted automatically after 12 months without Dünya play. "Reset progress" also deletes the Dünya record when the internet is available.

7. Your rights

For requests about your Dünya record (access, correction, deletion), the game code shown in Settings is all we need; we never ask for a name or e-mail. Purchase hashes and counter totals cannot be matched to anyone, so they cannot be found as a record belonging to a single person. For your rights under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and similar laws, write to habbe.uygulama@outlook.com.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and announce it in the game.

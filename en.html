Pofuduklar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026
Publisher: Wetrech Studio (Furkan Buğday). Contact: habbe.uygulama@outlook.com

1. In short

In Adventure and Home no information leaves the device unless you choose to watch an ad at the end of a match or to buy Elmas (section 3). Only when you enter Dünya is the minimum an online match needs processed, for the duration of the match, and deleted when the match ends (section 3a). Adventure and Home work without an internet connection.

2. Information stored on your device

  • The game stores some information only on your own device: game progress, plush collection, settings (sound, language) and an age group.
  • A match left unfinished is stored on the device so that it can be resumed where it stopped, and is deleted when the match ends.
  • The age group comes from the birth year chosen at first launch or from Google Play's age signal (section 4). The birth year itself is not stored. The age group is used only to choose age-appropriate settings.
  • We cannot see this information. If Android backup is on, Android may back it up to your own Google account (section 3).

3. Ads, purchases, backup, accounts

  • In-app purchases: Elmas packs are bought through Google Play. Your payment details stay with Google; we never see them. The purchase record signed by Google Play (order number and purchase token) is verified on the device and kept there with the fact that it was handled; it is not sent to any server of ours. For players under 13, or when no age has been given, an adult solves a short check before a purchase.
  • Rewarded ads: An ad is shown only at the end of a match, and only if you tap "Watch an ad", through Google AdMob; nothing is loaded before you tap, and there are no ads in Home matches or anywhere else in the game. Child-directed settings are on for every user: no personalised or remarketing ads, no advertising ID is sent (the app does not even have the advertising ID permission), no third-party ad measurement, and ad content is limited to the most general rating (G). When an ad is requested, Google processes, according to its own documentation: the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate the general location), interactions with the ad (views, taps, video views), diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the ads software, and device identifiers such as the app set ID. This data is encrypted in transit. How Google processes it: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Backup: If Android backup is on, Android may back up your game data (progress, collection, Elmas balance, cosmetics, settings) to your own Google account, and it comes back on a new phone or after a reinstall. We cannot access that backup; you can turn it off in Android settings.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Online play is only in Dünya and needs no account (section 3a).

3a. Dünya (online play)

  • The game connects to the internet only when you enter Dünya. Adventure and Home keep working offline.
  • For an online match we send only: a random game ID created at install (not your name, e-mail or phone number), the plush you picked, the four toys in your toy bag and a nickname chosen from a fixed list, your shot commands during the match and preset emotes.
  • Your game ID is held on the server only while your connection is open. So that a match can carry on where it left off if the connection drops, the match state is written to the server's temporary storage every turn. This record contains the nickname you chose from the fixed list, your plush and the toys in your bag, and which toys and maps the room has (those its creator has unlocked in the game); it contains no name, game ID, IP address or device information. When the match ends, all of its records are deleted. The server may run in Cloudflare's data centres in Europe; this information is processed there for the duration of the match. It is not stored permanently, not shared with anyone and not used for advertising or profiling.
  • The server runs on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Your IP address is processed to make the connection; we do not log it.
  • To stop too many wrong room-code attempts, an irreversible digest of the connection address is kept in memory for at most 15 minutes and is never written anywhere.
  • There is no chat and no free text. Players can only send preset emotes (such as a wave or a clap).
  • For players under 13, or when no age has been given, Dünya first asks for a short check that an adult solves.

4. Third parties

  • The game contains no analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • Rewarded ads use Google AdMob and purchases use Google Play Billing (section 3), both under Google's own terms and privacy policy.
  • The Dünya server runs on Cloudflare's infrastructure (section 3a). Cloudflare processes the connection only on our behalf, for the match; the server's logging is switched off.
  • At first launch the game asks the Google Play app on the device for an age range and parental approval status only (Google Play Age Signals). Google Play may ask you before sharing it. The answer is turned into an age group on the device; the game does not send it anywhere.
  • The store you downloaded the app from (Google Play) may process some download and device-stability information under its own terms and privacy policy. This processing is not under our control.

5. Children

  • Pofuduklar is suitable for players of all ages, including children.
  • Adventure and Home process no personal data. In Dünya, the IP address, a random game ID and a nickname are processed only to run the match, for the duration of the match. No profile is built, nothing is used for advertising, and it is deleted when the match ends. Before entering Dünya, players under 13 or without a given age get a short check that an adult solves; if Google Play reports that a parent declined, Dünya stays closed.
  • Ads are shown to everyone with child-directed settings, never personalised, and only when asked for (section 3). Before a real-money purchase, players under 13 or without a given age get a check that an adult solves.
  • If a future feature requires processing data, we will update this policy first and obtain parental consent where required.

6. Deletion

  • To delete all game data, uninstall the app or clear the app's data in your phone settings.
  • In-game Settings → "Reset progress" does the same.

7. Your rights

Because the information processed in Dünya (section 3a) is deleted when the match ends, we keep no lasting record about you. For your rights under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and similar laws (to be informed, to erasure and others), write to habbe.uygulama@outlook.com.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and announce it in the game.

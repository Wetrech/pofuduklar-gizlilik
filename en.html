Pofuduklar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026
Publisher: Wetrech Games (Furkan Buğday). Contact: habbe.uygulama@outlook.com

1. In short

Pofuduklar does not collect any personal data from you or your child and does not send any data anywhere. The game works without an internet connection.

2. Information stored on your device

  • The game stores some information only on your own device: game progress, plush collection, settings (sound, language) and an age group.
  • The age group comes from the birth year chosen at first launch or from Google Play's age signal (section 4). The birth year itself is not stored. The age group is used only to choose age-appropriate settings.
  • This information never leaves the device, and nobody, including us, can access it.

3. Ads, purchases, accounts

This version has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts or sign-in, no chat and no online play.

4. Third parties

  • The game contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • At first launch the game asks the Google Play app on the device for an age range and parental approval status only (Google Play Age Signals). Google Play may ask you before sharing it. The answer is turned into an age group on the device; the game does not send it anywhere.
  • The store you downloaded the app from (Google Play) may process some download and device-stability information under its own terms and privacy policy. This processing is not under our control.

5. Children

  • Pofuduklar is suitable for players of all ages, including children.
  • Because we do not collect personal data, no parental consent for the collection of children's data is required.
  • If a future feature requires processing data, we will update this policy first and obtain parental consent where required.

6. Deletion

  • To delete all game data, uninstall the app or clear the app's data in your phone settings.
  • In-game Settings → "Reset progress" does the same.

7. Your rights

As we do not process personal data, we hold no records about you under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or similar laws. Send questions to habbe.uygulama@outlook.com.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and announce it in the game.
